About this Species

Clownfish are famed for their remarkable symbiotic relationship with sea anemones. These vibrant orange fish are born with a specialized mucus coating that protects them from the anemone's stinging tentacles, allowing them to nestle safely within their host. In exchange for protection from predators, the clownfish provides nutrients and lures prey to the anemone. Their iconic white patterns, framed by delicate black outlines, make them one of the most recognizable and beloved residents of the Indo-Pacific coral reefs.
